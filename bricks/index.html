Print Your Own LegoDesign a piece, then download it or order a real print.

Sizes in mm: 8 mm stud pitch, 4.8 mm stud diameter. STL files have no colour, so pick your colour when you order.

Order a real print

Download your STL, then upload it to one of these print services. They print it and post it straight to you, in Australia or worldwide. You pay the service directly.

  • Craftcloud: compares prices from many printers, ships worldwide
  • Treatstock: instant quotes from print shops, ships worldwide
  • JLC3DP: low cost resin and nylon prints, ships to Australia
